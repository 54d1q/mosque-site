Our Vision

To create a vibrant, welcoming, and spiritual hub that serves the religious, social, and educational needs of the Muslim community in Southampton, while fostering understanding and harmony with the wider society.

"You are the best community ever raised for humanity—you encourage good, forbid evil, and believe in Allah." (Surah Al-Imran, 3:110)

Core Islamic Values

Our center is built upon the timeless principles of Islam. We strive to embody these values in every service we provide:

  • Ikhlas (Sincerity)

    We perform all our actions purely for the pleasure of Allah (SWT).

  • Ilm (Knowledge)

    We believe education is the foundation of a strong community and encourage lifelong learning.

  • Adl (Justice)

    We are committed to fairness, integrity, and transparency in all our dealings.

  • Rahmah (Compassion)

    We serve humanity with kindness, empathy, and a helping hand for those in need.

  • Khidmah (Service)

    We see leadership as service to the people, working tirelessly to support our neighbors.

Community Pledge
We pledge to maintain an environment that is safe, inclusive, and respectful for all, regardless of background or ethnicity.
