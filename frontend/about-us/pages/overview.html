A Brief Overview

“ Our mission is to Serve, Educate and Inspire through faith and action. ”

The Shahjalal Mosque & Islamic Center, which serves the Southampton community, has a rich history, with roots established decades ago. It has gone on to become one of the most active Islamic institutions in the region, serving as a hub for prayer, learning, and community cohesion.

Our Services

We offer a wide range of services from our complex to support the needs of our diverse community. In addition to the daily prayers and Jumu'ah, we provide:

  • Advice and Counselling
  • Education for Children and Adults
  • Marriage Services
  • Funeral & Death Services
  • Community Events & Charity
