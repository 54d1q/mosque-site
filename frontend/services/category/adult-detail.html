Adult Education

Our Adult Education programs are designed to provide spiritual growth and knowledge for the community.

  • Halaqah (Study Circle) Every Tuesday at 10:00 PM

    Our weekly Halaqah is an interactive study circle where we explore the depths of Islamic Fiqh (jurisprudence) and Seerah (prophetic history). This session is designed to bridge traditional knowledge with modern living, allowing attendees to discuss practical applications of faith in their daily lives. It is a great opportunity to ask questions, share insights, and grow together in a relaxed, supportive environment. Food will be provided at the end of the session.

  • Darusul Quran Every Sunday after Maghrib

    The Darusul Quran sessions focus on the core connection between a believer and the Book of Allah. These lessons cover proper Tajweed (rules of recitation) to ensure the Quran is read correctly, followed by a Tafseer (exegesis) segment. We delve into the meanings and context of specific Surahs, helping you understand the message of the Quran and how to implement its guidance into your character and actions.

  • Darusul Hadith Mon - Thu after Zuhr

    Held daily between Monday and Thursday, these short but powerful sessions are dedicated to the study of Hadith, primarily focusing on the famous compilation 'Riadus-Salihin' (The Meadows of the Righteous). We examine the sayings and traditions of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) to refine our manners, strengthen our spirituality, and emulate his noble example in our community dealings.

Note: These services are male only
