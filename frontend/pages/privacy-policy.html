Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect student details (Name, DOB, Address), parent contact information, and attendance records for administrative purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To manage classroom attendance and student safety.
  • To process fee payments via Stripe.
  • To send important school updates via email.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to manage your login session. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

4. Data Security

Student data is stored in an encrypted database. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access is restricted to authorized staff only.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your data. Please contact the Mosque Admin for any requests.
